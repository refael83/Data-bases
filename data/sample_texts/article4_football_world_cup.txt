TITLE: Argentina Defeats France in Thrilling World Cup Final
AUTHOR: Carlos Mendez
NEWSPAPER: BBC News
DATE: 2025-12-18
PAGE: 1
TOPIC: Sports
LANGUAGE: en
---
In one of the greatest matches in football history, Argentina defeated France 4-2 on penalties to win the FIFA World Cup, sending millions of fans into jubilant celebrations across Buenos Aires and beyond.

The match, played before a packed crowd of 88,000 at Lusail Stadium in Qatar, lived up to its billing as a clash between two of the sports biggest stars: Lionel Messi of Argentina and Kylian Mbappe of France.

Messi, widely regarded as the greatest player of his generation, opened the scoring in the 23rd minute with a penalty kick that sent French goalkeeper Hugo Lloris the wrong way. Angel Di Maria doubled Argentinas lead with a brilliant team goal in the 36th minute, capping off a flowing move that involved 15 passes.

France appeared to be heading for a comprehensive defeat, but Mbappe produced one of the most remarkable comebacks in World Cup history. The Paris Saint-Germain striker scored twice in 97 seconds late in the second half, first from the penalty spot and then with a stunning volley that left Argentine goalkeeper Emiliano Martinez rooted to the spot.

The match went to extra time, where both teams traded blows. Messi thought he had won the game when he scored from close range in the 108th minute, but Mbappe completed his hat trick with another penalty in the 118th minute to force a shootout.

In the penalty shootout, Argentina held their nerve while France faltered. Martinez saved one attempt and Aurelien Tchouameni missed the target entirely, giving Argentina a 4-2 victory from the spot.

"This is the moment I have dreamed of my entire life," Messi said as he lifted the golden trophy, tears streaming down his face. "I want to dedicate this to my family, my teammates, and to all of Argentina."

The victory sparked massive celebrations across Argentina. An estimated five million people took to the streets of Buenos Aires, turning the city into a sea of blue and white. President Alberto Fernandez declared a national holiday, and schools and businesses closed for the day.

French President Emmanuel Macron consoled the defeated players in the locker room after the match. "You gave everything tonight," he told them. "France is proud of you."

For Mbappe, who scored a hat trick in a losing effort, there was consolation in winning the Golden Boot as the tournaments top scorer with eight goals.

The match has been widely praised by pundits and former players as the greatest World Cup final ever played. "I have never seen anything like it," said former England captain Gary Lineker. "That was beyond sport. That was drama at its absolute finest."

As the celebrations continue in Buenos Aires, one thing is certain: this World Cup final will be remembered for generations to come.
